Section 01

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Famzgames ("we", "us", "our") handles information in the Brain Dash Android application ("the App") available on Google Play Store.

Brain Dash uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. As a result, certain data may be collected by Google to serve and optimize ads. This policy explains what data is collected, by whom, and how it is used.

By downloading or using Brain Dash, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the App.

Developer vs. AdMob:

Famzgames itself collects no personal data. However, Google AdMob — as a third-party advertising service — may collect data independently as described in this policy. We are required to disclose this to you.

Children's Privacy & COPPA

Brain Dash is designed for mixed ages including children. We take children's privacy seriously and comply with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and Google Play's Families Policy.

⚠️ Important — Ads & Children:

Because Brain Dash targets mixed ages (including under 13), we are required to configure AdMob to serve non-personalized, child-directed ads only for all users, or to implement age-gating. Brain Dash is configured to request non-personalized ads to comply with COPPA and Google Play Families Policy.

Our commitments for child safety:

  • ✓

  • ✓

  • ✓

  • ✓

  • ✓
